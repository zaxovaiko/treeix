Review requested from you Mark stays until GitHub records a review from you

Where "reviewed" comes from

  • GitHub: your latest review on the PR (gh pr list --json reviews,reviewRequests). Approved, Changes requested and Commented all count.
  • GitLab: your approval, or a discussion note you left.
  • Local "Mark as reviewed" for PRs you skimmed without submitting a review; stored per PR head commit.

When it comes back

  • New commits pushed after your review (or after your local mark) put the PR back in front, labeled with the count.
  • A new review request from the author does the same.
  • Your own PRs are treated as "nothing to review" and fade with the reviewed ones.

Trade-offs

  • A keeps list order and position; cheapest to scan but reviewed rows still take space.
  • B makes the to-do explicit and shrinks reviewed rows to one line; order changes as state changes.
  • C shows who else reviewed and hides the noise entirely; hidden rows are easy to forget.
